# A9 session 1: the two native families (2026-11-06)

NOD-9 session 1 on the Classic, branch `feat/a9-natives`. Both native gaps A8
found are closed, and the live Prober counter now runs through the app's own
`OnClickListener` instead of the launcher's fallback counter.

## What changed

* **NativeConverter**: the stock `libcore_icu_NativeConverter.cpp` on the
  sysroot ICU 4.6 (already linked, converter tables and aliases included),
  registered in the slot of `src/icu_stubs.cc`, which is deleted. Patch 0103
  replaces the one construct newer than 4.6 (`icu::UStringEnumeration` in
  `collectStandardNames`) with the C enumeration, as patch 0100 does for ICU.
* **StaticLayout**: the 12 `android.text.StaticLayout` natives in
  `src/graphics_facade.cc`, as a greedy line breaker over the FreeType widths.
  Breaks after space runs, a forced break inside a word wider than the line,
  tab stops, indents, no hyphenation, every strategy greedy. Widths come from
  `charAdvances()`, which `advance()` now sums too, so line breaking and
  measuring cannot disagree. Control chars measure 0.
* **Launcher live**: `sBump.performClick()` (the Prober's listener) replaces
  the fallback counter; the layout pass runs only when `isLayoutRequested()`.
* **`run_core.sh live`**: the A8 live command line, versioned (it was typed by
  hand before). Log in `out/live.log`.

  class android/util/Log": the framework JNI needs framework.dex): NOD-24.

redraws ending at `count: 9` on the panel. No click outside the button rect,
no `UnsatisfiedLinkError`, no fatal.
